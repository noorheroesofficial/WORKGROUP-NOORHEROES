## 1. Objective and Purpose
Provide an interactive tour of the NoorHeroes curriculum modules and showcase Islamic scholarly accreditation and COPPA child-safety safeguards.

## 2. Technical Features
- `CurriculumTabs`: Interactive tab switcher covering Stories, Quran, Duas, Habits, Quizzes, and Gamification with autoplaying webp video snippets.
- `ScholarAdvisoryGrid`: Verified profiles of Islamic education scholars who review curriculum content.
- `SafetyFeatureGrid`: 4 key trust pillars (100% Ad-Free, No Open Chat, Zero Biometric Storage, Hardware-Backed Privacy).
